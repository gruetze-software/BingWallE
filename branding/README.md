# BingWallE artwork

The approved master is `bingwalle.png` (1254 × 1254, RGBA, transparent outside the icon).
It was generated with the built-in Imagegen tool and approved on 15 October 2026.
The earlier flat SVG logo is retired; it remains available in Git history.

Run `python scripts/generate-icons.py` from the repository root with Pillow installed.
This derives the 256 px Linux/header PNG, 1024 px information-dialog PNG, Windows
ICO (16–256 px) and macOS ICNS. It preserves the approved artwork and transparency.
Do not regenerate the master from the old SVG.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: new desktop app icon and logo concept for BingWallE, a tasteful Bing wallpaper collection app for Windows, macOS and Linux. Generate ONE standalone square app icon, centered, with genuinely transparent space outside its rounded-square silhouette. No presentation board, no mockup, no lettering, no captions or watermark. Creative direction: a miniature landscape sculpted from broad elegantly folded layers of paper, merging a photographic landscape with the idea of a carefully kept collection. Bold dark petrol rounded-square outer body; inside, two sweeping angular teal and turquoise mountain folds create a memorable W-like landscape silhouette, a deep blue winding river cutout leads into the distance, and a warm golden sun rises behind the peaks. A very subtle offset second photo edge at the upper left hints at collecting wallpapers, integrated into the silhouette. Premium contemporary desktop icon design, confident simple geometric forms, restrained tactile depth with softly beveled paper edges, beautiful nuanced teal and amber lighting, exceptionally crisp anti-aliased contours. Richer character than a generic flat photo placeholder, yet legible at 32 pixels. Keep only a few large shapes; no tiny scenic details, no foliage, no stars, no extra badges. Front facing, balanced generous inset spacing, strong contrast. High-resolution polished raster logo concept suitable for later production icon assets. The icon should occupy about 88 percent of the canvas. Transparent background outside icon, no cast shadow spreading beyond the icon silhouette.
